- `dist/assets/tongji-identity.png`: the original university emblem and wordmark reference published by Tongji University News, https://news.tongji.edu.cn/info/1008/94580.htm . Original image: https://news.tongji.edu.cn/__local/5/A7/F1/E3C5397E909F96DCCB79D82EF3C_7AE4B728_79D6.png . CSS displays the complete circular emblem at the left of that reference; the downloaded file is preserved unchanged.

The small emblems identify the universities in the education section and link to their official websites.
